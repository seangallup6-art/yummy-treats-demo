Bottom sheet, phone only. Batch Apply: "Show 12 results".
Motion. Hover lifts 2 px and grows the shadow; active presses 2 px and drops it. Hero eases in on load; cards rise 16 px on scroll, staggered 60 ms. One marquee per page, 22 s per loop, with a Pause button; it stops under prefers-reduced-motion. Toast lasts 2.2 s. Backdrop shapes: off by default for Billboard, "Subtle" adds outlined stars and sparkles in ink at 8% on the yellow.
Spacing and shape. 8 px grid; section gaps 72 px desktop, 48 px phone; page gutters 48 px desktop, 20 px phone. Borders 3 px everywhere, 2 px on phone. Corners: pills for anything you press, 20 px for cards, 12 px for inputs, 24 px for big blocks. Shadows 4 px (buttons), 6 px (cards), 8 px (hero blocks); 3 px on phone.
Icons and images. Stroke icons at 2 px, ink. Product images on tinted panels in the palette family (yellow, pink, cobalt tints at 20%), outlined packs in the illustrations. Photos get a 3 px ink border and a corner. No photo carries a text label; captions sit below.
