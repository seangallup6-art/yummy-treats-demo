  • product-card badge logic ported to a PHP template tag, same five states.
  • css-variables fluid type and Thai factor become theme.json fontSizes plus a :lang(th) rule.
  • delivery-table, payment-methods sections become patterns on Help.
  • backdrop-shapes kept as an Off / Subtle setting; Billboard ships Off.
